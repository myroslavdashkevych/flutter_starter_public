Privacy policy

Flutter Starter. Last updated: November 2, 2026.

This policy explains what data the Flutter Starter mobile app collects, why, how long it is kept and what choices you have.

Who is responsible

The developer of Flutter Starter is responsible for your data. Contact: support@example.com. Google provides the Firebase services listed below and processes this data on the developer's behalf.

All of this data is encrypted in transit. It is not sold and not used for advertising.

Data that stays on your device

Your favorites, the cached list of items, and your theme, language and data sharing settings are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app removes them.

Content from other services

The list of items and their images come from the public Rick and Morty API. Like any website, this service receives your IP address when the app loads content from it. The app sends it no account data.

Your choices

  • Usage data. Sharing is on by default. Turn off "Share usage data" in Settings to stop Firebase Analytics collection and crash reports, including the session data sent with them. It works without an account and takes effect immediately, with one exception: a crash in the app's native code during the same session can still be reported. From the next launch, nothing from these services is sent.
  • Notifications. Turn them off in the system settings of your device. "Share usage data" does not affect them, because delivering a notification is not data collection for analysis.
  • Your account. Delete your account and its data in the app or by email: see how to delete your account.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, and to object to or restrict its processing. To use these rights, email support@example.com. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes too.
