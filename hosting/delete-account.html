Delete your account

Flutter Starter, by its developer. Last updated: November 2, 2026.

In the app

  1. Open the Profile tab and sign in if needed.
  2. Tap Delete account and read the warning.
  3. Enter your password to confirm.

The account is deleted right away.

By email, without the app

Send a request to support@example.com from the email address of your account, with the subject "Delete my account - Flutter Starter". The request is handled within 30 days, and you get a confirmation by email.

What is deleted

  • Your Firebase Authentication account: email address, password and user ID. It is removed from backups within 180 days.
  • Your favorites on the device, when you delete the account in the app.

What is kept

  • Crash reports, which may contain your user ID: up to 90 days.
  • Usage statistics, which are not linked to your account: up to 14 months.
  • The cached list of items and settings on your device, which hold no account data, until you uninstall the app.

To also remove your Firebase Installation ID, which is used to deliver notifications, mention it in an email request. It is removed from backups within 180 days.
